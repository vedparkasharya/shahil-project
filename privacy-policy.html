Lite PDF Viewer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Lite PDF Viewer is designed to open and display PDF documents locally on your Android device.

Information collection

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It does not include advertising, analytics, account registration, or remote tracking.

Files and permissions

When you select or share a PDF, Android grants the app temporary access to that document so it can display the pages. PDF content is processed on your device and is not uploaded to our servers by this app.

Data retention

The app does not operate a server and does not retain copies of your documents. Android and other apps may independently manage the original files.

Children

The app is a general-purpose document viewer and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Keep your device and Android software updated. Do not open documents from sources you do not trust.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if app functionality changes. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the contact details shown on the Google Play listing.
